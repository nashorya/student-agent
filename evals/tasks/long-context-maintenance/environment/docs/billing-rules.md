# Billing Rules

- Paused accounts are skipped from renewal reporting.
- Usage is measured in GB and rounded up before overage calculation.
- Extra seats cost ¥8 per seat.
- Tax is applied after discounts.
- Tax rates are EU 20%, US 8%, APAC 10%.
- Trial accounts receive a 50% pre-tax discount.
- Renewal reports should show account name, plan name, final total, and flags.
